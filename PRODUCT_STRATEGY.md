# SignalForge AI: Product Strategy & Scaling Roadmap

## 1. Vision: The Autonomous Content Engine
SignalForge is moving from a utility tool to an ecosystem. We solve the "Blank Page" problem by identifying what happens *before* the prompt (Trend Detection) and what happens *after* the generation (Distribution & Optimization).

## 2. Competitive Advantage: The Multi-Agent Advantage
Unlike competitors who use single-prompt wrappers, SignalForge uses a sequential agent chain:
- **Research Agent**: Deep web scraping and fact-gathering.
- **Trend Intelligence**: Social listening for high-virality hooks.
- **Content Architect**: Platform-specific structural design.
- **Brand Guard**: Ensures 100% adherence to voice and legal compliance.

## 3. Monetization Roadmap
- **Starter ($49/mo)**: 100 Forged Assets, 1 Workspace.
- **Pro ($149/mo)**: Unlimited Multi-Agent workflows, Trend Alerts, 5 Workspaces.
- **Agency ($499/mo)**: White-labeling, Client Approval Portals, API Access.
- **Enterprise**: Custom LLM fine-tuning on brand history.

## 4. Scaling Horizon
- **V1 (Current)**: Manual input to content generation.
- **V2 (Immediate)**: Workspace-aware autonomous trend detection.
- **V3 (Next)**: Direct social API integrations (Buffer/Zapier) & closed-loop analytics.
